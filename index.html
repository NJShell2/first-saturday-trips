How ranking works

Every event is scored out of 100 by a script, no judgment calls: daytime fit (25), drive time (20, shorter is better, over 3 hours is dropped), cost (20, free is best), ticket availability (15, verified not sold out is best), and quality (10, from Ticketmaster popularity or the event's scale). The top five are then spread across categories so you do not get five concerts. Hotel rooms are only shown as verified when a live check confirmed a king room for that Friday night; otherwise the card links you to check availability yourself.

Events are re-checked every night at midnight. When the next first Saturday rolls around, this page rolls with it.
